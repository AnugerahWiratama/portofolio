## Brand & Style
The brand personality reflects architectural precision, restrained luxury, and curated intellectual poise. Built for a multi-disciplinary designer and creative technologist, this portfolio balances archival rigor with tactile editorial refinement. 

The aesthetic fuses **Swiss Modernism** and **Architectural Editorial Minimalism**:
- **Monolithic Balance:** Massive scale contrast between stark, tall condensed headlines and delicate, classic serif pull-quotes.
- **Architectural Rhythm:** Disciplined structural alignment, framing content with subtle tectonic hairlines instead of heavy volumetric cards.
- **Museum Restraint:** Vast planes of negative space, allowing artifacts, typography, and case studies to breathe without decorative clutter or synthetic gradients.
- **Emotional Imprint:** Elicits confidence, deliberate authority, calmness, and high artistic curation.

## Colors
The palette is grounded in warm architectural stone, raw masonry, and high-density carbon ink.

- **Primary (`#181818` / Charcoal Ink):** The anchor for high-impact typography, core navigation elements, filled action buttons, and dominant structural contours.
- **Secondary (`#686762` / Muted Warm Gray):** Utilized for metadata, captions, section indexes, running headers, and secondary typographic layers.
- **Tertiary (`#E2E1DB` / Subtle Darker Stone):** Dedicated to recessed surface containers, subtle hover fills, segmented pill tracks, and alternating case study index bands.
- **Neutral (`#EAE9E4` / Warm Stone Canvas):** The foundational canvas that replaces sterile digital white with a tactile, limestone paper warmth.

### Hairline Borders
Structural division relies on an ink wash stroke (`rgba(24, 24, 24, 0.14)`). It delineates structural grids and column tracks without introducing visual heaviness.

## Typography
Typographic hierarchy is built on intentional tension between structural industrialism, classical editorial grace, and utilitarian precision.

- **Display & Headlines (Oswald):** Set with tight vertical metrics, capitalized title weights, and condensed proportions to evoke architectural scaffolds and mid-century editorial covers.
- **Editorial Sub-voices (Playfair Display):** Introduced for pull quotes, project narratives, design philosophy statements, and contextual annotations. Adds organic literary warmth to mechanical structure.
- **Functional Body & Interface (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Grounded, neutral, and highly legible across screen sizes. Handles long-form documentation, project specifications, and micro-copy.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a modular 12-column architectural grid grounded in absolute column alignments and unyielding margins.

- **Grid Architecture:** 12 columns on desktop (above 1024px) with `2.5rem` gutters and `4rem` outer canvas margins. 4 columns on mobile (<768px) with `1.5rem` gutters and margins.
- **Section Rhythm:** Generous vertical intervals (`space-xl` scaled to `6rem` or `8rem` for major section dividers) create an open, exhibition-like cadence.
- **Hairline Framing:** Visual boundaries rely on continuous `1px` lines (`rgba(24, 24, 24, 0.14)`) running edge-to-edge across the grid canvas, bounding full-bleed image bays and project listings.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is strictly planimetric and planar. The system rejects faux realism, drop shadows, and synthetic blur filters.

- **Zero-Shadow Rule:** Interface elements rely on tectonic stacking, contrast shifts, and physical bounding rules rather than cast shadows.
- **Tonal Stepping:** Overlays and flyout drawers utilize `#E2E1DB` or deep `#181818` surfaces to contrast sharply against the `#EAE9E4` canvas.
- **Tectonic Inset Lines:** Depth within modules is conveyed by enclosing zones in `1px` architectural hairlines (`rgba(24,24,24,0.14)`), mimicking technical blueprint frames.

## Shapes
Shapes employ a sharp dual philosophy: **rigid architectural frames** contrasted against **hyper-sculpted organic interaction pills**.

- **Cards, Frames, and Imagery:** Strict `0px` radius (sharp corners) to honor brutalist editorial grids and print paper sheets.
- **Interactive Pills (`roundedness: 3`):** Buttons, interactive tags, category filters, and status badges use fully rounded pill geometries (e.g., `rounded-full` / `9999px`). This creates immediate affordance for touch and click surfaces amidst the rectilinear grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill Button:** Solid `#181818` background, `#EAE9E4` text (`label-caps`), zero border, `9999px` border-radius. Padding: `0.75rem 2rem`. Micro-interaction: On hover, scales slightly (`transform: scale(1.02)`), shifts background to `#686762`, or triggers an inverted pill expansion with an arrow icon.
- **Secondary Outlined Pill:** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(24, 24, 24, 0.2)` border, `#181818` text. Hover fills the pill with `#E2E1DB`.
- **Text Action:** `label-caps` typography underlined with a `1px` offset stroke that animates in width on cursor proximity.

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- Fully rounded pills with subtle stone fill (`#E2E1DB`), `label-caps` text set in `#686762`, with `0.375rem 0.875rem` padding.
- Selected state flips to solid `#181818` with stone `#EAE9E4` typography.

### Lists & Index Rows
- Editorial archive table format: continuous full-width rows bordered by a top `1px` hairline (`rgba(24,24,24,0.14)`).
- Hovering a list row alters the entire row background to `#E2E1DB` with zero transition lag, revealing preview imagery in an architectural popover.

### Cards & Project Folios
- Razor-sharp corners (`0px`).
- Framed with `1px` borders or defined strictly by whitespace gaps between columns.
- Media elements display pure image crops with a subtle `1px` internal matte outline. Captions use `editorial-italic` or `label-caps`.

### Input Fields
- Understated architectural lines: transparent background with a single bottom border (`1px solid rgba(24,24,24,0.3)`).
- On focus, border becomes a crisp `1px solid #181818` with no glow or halo. Label sits as `label-caps` above the baseline.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Crisp, unrounded boxes for checkboxes (`0px` radius) and concentric circles for radios.
- Borders use `1.5px solid #181818`, with a solid `#181818` square or dot center fill when active.
